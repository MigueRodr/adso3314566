📌 How to use your own audio:

  1. Replace your-audio-file.mp3 with the path to your audio file
  2. Supported formats: MP3, OGG, WAV, AAC, M4A
  3. Example: src="music/song.mp3" or src="../audio/my-recording.ogg"
  4. Use the upload button to play local audio files directly
