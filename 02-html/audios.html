📌 How to use your own video:

  1. Replace your-video-file.mp4 with the path to your video file
  2. Supported formats: MP4, WebM, OGV, MOV, AVI (browser dependent)
  3. Example: src="videos/my-movie.mp4" or src="../media/intro.webm"
  4. Use the upload button to play local video files directly
  5. For better compatibility, provide multiple formats (MP4, WebM, OGV)

🎬 Recommended video attributes:

  • controls - Shows play/pause, volume, and timeline controls
  • autoplay - Starts playing automatically (often needs muted)
  • loop - Repeats the video indefinitely
  • muted - Starts with sound off (required for autoplay in most browsers)
  • poster="image.jpg" - Shows a preview image before playing
